What you get in Odoo

Screens from a connected Odoo, shown with sample data.

Calls list

Every call in one list

One row per call, newest first, for every call that passes through Voxbi.

  • Initiated by, recipient, start and end time, duration
  • Answered or missed badge, how the call came in, and the AI topic
  • Filters: My Calls, Today and Missed
  • Search by caller name, recipient name or number
  • Known numbers highlighted apart from unknown ones
  • Calls Analysis: call minutes by month, inbound vs outbound
  • Works with Odoo VoIP (Enterprise): calls placed from Odoo are logged like any other call
Call record

The full picture on every call

Who was on the line, how the call ran, and what was said.

  • Name, number, email and linked Odoo contact of the other party
  • Duration, start, answer and end time, billable seconds
  • The queue or extension the call came through
  • Every participant with name, number and role, transfers included
  • Tabs for Transcription, Translation and Notes
AI analysis

AI analysis built in

Get the gist of a call without listening to it.

  • One-line topic and a written summary
  • Keywords and colour-coded labels
  • Sentiment and NPS with score, category and reasoning
  • Speakers, discussion points, agreements and action items
Actions

From call to work in one click

Buttons at the top of every call.

  • Save to Contacts creates the contact from an unknown number
  • Open Contact jumps to the matched contact
  • Create Lead, Opportunity, Ticket or Task, pre-filled and linked back to the call
  • Each button shows only for users of that app
Linking

Calls where the work lives

Call history on the record your team is already working on.

  • Leads, Opportunities, Tickets and Tasks tabs on the call
  • Link / Linked toggle per row, unlink a mistake from the record
  • Calls related through the contact appear automatically
  • Call tabs on contacts, leads, opportunities, helpdesk tickets and project tasks
Contacts

Every call on the contact

A Voxbi Calls tab on each contact lists every call with them.

  • Calls appear as soon as they are matched to the contact
  • Same columns as the calls list: who called, when, duration, status and topic
  • Users see the calls they handled; administrators see them all
Leads, opportunities and tasks

Calls on every record

Leads, opportunities, project tasks and helpdesk tickets each get two call tabs.

  • All Calls: every call with the record's customer
  • Linked Calls: the calls linked to this record, with Unlink to undo a mistake
  • Records created or linked from a call show it here straight away
Helpdesk · Enterprise

Calls on every ticket

With Odoo Helpdesk, tickets get the same All Calls and Linked Calls tabs.

  • Every call with the customer, right on the ticket
  • Create Ticket on a call opens a ticket already linked to it
  • Linked tickets show on the call's Tickets tab

Transparent connection. Explicit authorization.

This module connects Odoo to Voxbi Cockpit, an external service operated by VOXBI S.A. You authorize the connection on Voxbi's own sign-in screen, and the details below are sent over HTTPS.

Sent to Voxbi Cockpit
  • Your Odoo URL, database name and Odoo version
  • The login of the administrator who connects, with a temporary Odoo API key that Voxbi uses to set up the integration
  • Once setup succeeds, the login and API key of a dedicated, non-administrator Voxbi Sync user, which replaces the administrator's key for the ongoing call sync

Revoke a key at any time in Preferences → Account Security, or disconnect from Voxbi → Configuration. You keep ownership of your data. A Voxbi account and an active Voxbi subscription are required. Privacy policy: voxbi.com/legal/privacy-policy
